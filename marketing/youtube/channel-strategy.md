# TableLab — Faceless Brand Channel Strategy (90-Day)

*Channel type: Niche Authority · Tier: New (0 subs / 0 videos) · Resource: solo · Goal: growth → app installs*

Proof of concept: GTO Wizard runs a faceless, product-branded, education-first poker channel
(now the WSOP's official training partner) — a proven format for this niche.

## Positioning Statement

> **TableLab helps small- and mid-stakes poker players turn their own tracked results into a
> study plan — through faceless, solver-grounded breakdowns that show exactly which leaks are
> costing them money and how to fix them — a loop no tracker or trainer channel currently owns.**

**Breakdown**
- **Unique angle** — the *track → diagnose → study* loop. Start from a player's own results, show which leak to fix first.
- **Target viewer** — the NL2–NL100 cash / small-buy-in MTT player who logs sessions (or knows they should) and is overwhelmed by abstract solver content.
- **Differentiation** — GTO Wizard and the coach channels teach strategy *in the abstract*; the tracker tools (PokerTracker, Hold'em Manager) have almost **zero** content presence. Nobody owns "your stats reveal the leak → here's the solver-backed fix."
- **Micro-niche start** — "results-tracking + leak-fixing for small-stakes cash players." Dominate with 30–40 videos before expanding.
- **Expansion path** — 10K subs: add tournament/ICM. 50K: live/mid-stakes. 100K: general strategy authority.

## Content Pillars (Hub / Hero / Help)

Target mix: **~55% Help, ~30% Hub, ~15% Hero.**

**Pillar 1 — "Fix This Leak" · HELP · Search** (~50%)
Evergreen how-tos, each targeting one keyword cluster, grounded in the GTO Explorer / solver library.
1. Bankroll Management: How Many Buy-Ins You Actually Need *(repurpose live blog post)*
2. Stop Calling Rivers With Bluff-Catchers — The Pot-Odds Math
3. The 3 Leaks Costing Small-Stakes Players the Most Money

**Pillar 2 — "Read Your Own Stats" · HUB · Browse/Subscribers** (~30%)
Recurring series that IS the product loop — walk a (synthetic) tracked player from a number to a study action.
1. Your Win Rate Is Trying to Tell You Something (BB/100 explained)
2. What a Losing Month Actually Means — Variance vs Leak
3. Monthly Session Review: Turning Results Into a Study List

**Pillar 3 — "Solver Says" · HELP/HERO · Search+Suggested** (~15%)
Punchy solver-truth explainers using GTO Explorer visuals — hard for creators to replicate.
1. Is Limping Ever GTO? (What the Solver Actually Does)
2. BTN vs BB: The Flop Strategy Solvers Agree On
3. Can AI Actually Solve Poker? *(repurpose blog post)*

**Pillar 4 (optional) — "Hand Coach" · HERO · Suggested** (~5%, tentpole)
Deep single-hand breakdowns using the AI hand coach + equity cross-check, showing the reasoning.

**Shorts** — one solver truth / one stat / one leak per Short, sliced from all four pillars. Discovery funnel.

## Upload Cadence

**1 long-form + 3 Shorts per week** to start (ramp to 2 long-form ~day 60 once a pipeline exists).

Rationale — template baseline is 2 long-form/week, but you're a solo founder also running the product,
and every video must be solver-accurate (quality is the differentiator). 1 excellent search-targeted
video beats 2 rushed ones; Shorts carry discovery while the long-form library compounds.

| Day | Activity |
|---|---|
| Mon | Keyword + solver research for the week's long-form |
| Tue–Wed | Script + screen-record app/Explorer |
| Thu | Edit + VO + metadata package |
| Fri | Publish long-form |
| Daily | 1 Short from the backlog (3/wk) |

## 30 / 60 / 90-Day Milestones

- **Day 30** — 4–5 long-form + ~12 Shorts · 50–150 subs · identity locked (banner, About + UTM'd app link, consistent thumbnail palette) · install funnel instrumented.
- **Day 60** — ~9 long-form · 200–500 subs · first video >1K views · **installs attributed to YouTube UTM** · production system documented.
- **Day 90** — ~13 long-form · 500–1K subs · one video >5K views · repeatable pipeline · clear read on which pillar drives installs.

*(New faceless channels are genuinely slow — realistic, not sandbagged. Search-compounding channels look flat for months, then inflect.)*

Add the channel's `sameAs` URL to the `#organization` node (duplicated across `about.html`, `blog/index.html`, both blog posts — edit all copies together).
