# Blog Strategy: TableLab

> Built on `BRAND.md` + `VOICE.md` (project root) and the `tablelab` persona. Last updated: 2026-06-29.
> Primary goal: **organic authority + AI citations** (SEO long-game). Cadence: **~1 post/week**. Mix: **~70% owned / 30% earned**.

## Executive Summary

TableLab builds topical authority in two arenas live players actually search — **bankroll/results discipline** and **honest, solver-grounded strategy** — and earns AI-engine citations by answering the questions competitors answer dishonestly or not at all. The wedge against the category leader (GTO Wizard) is threefold: **live-first** (not online/MTT theory), **radically honest about what AI does vs. what a CFR solver does**, and **tied to the reader's own tracked results** (which no solver/study tool can do). We build two clusters deep in 90 days rather than scattering posts, leading with a flagship credibility piece to set the honest tone before the search-volume cluster.

## Audience

Per `BRAND.md`: serious recreational-to-semi-pro **live** cash + MTT players who track results — numerate, advanced, and allergic to AI hype. Two working segments:

- **The Tracker** — already logs sessions; wants to know their *true* win rate, bankroll requirements, variance reality. High commercial intent (product-aligned). Searches: "good live poker win rate," "how many buy-ins bankroll," "poker variance calculator."
- **The Skeptic-Studier** — curious about GTO/solvers for live but distrusts "AI coaching." Searches/asks AI engines: "can AI solve poker," "is GTO Wizard AI legit," "what is a poker solver," "GTO for live games." This is the AI-citation goldmine — honest, structured answers are scarce.

## Positioning & Competitive Wedge

| Competitor set | What they own | Our wedge |
|---|---|---|
| **Live trackers** (Poker Bankroll Tracker, Poker Income, RunGood, Pokerbase, Poker Analytics) | Tracking UX; thin/no content marketing | We out-*content* them: tie every strategy/bankroll concept back to the data the tracker already holds |
| **GTO study tools** (GTO Wizard blog/academy, raw solvers Pio/TexasSolver) | Deep theory content; strong SERP + AI presence; "GTO Wizard AI" branding | Live-first, not online-range theory; **honest about the LLM/solver split**; we *track your results*, they don't |
| **Bankroll/variance content** (BlackRain79, PokerCoaching, PLO Mastermind, calculators) | Generic + online-skewed bankroll guides | Live-specific buy-in math, and "stop guessing — here's what *your* sample says" |

**The honesty angle is itself a moat.** GTO Wizard markets "GTO Wizard AI" (a solver-backed neural net) in language that blurs into "AI solves poker." The market is confused, and a numerate audience resents the blur. TableLab's *Honest Layer* content — "a language model is not a solver; here's exactly what each does for your hand" — is differentiated, citable, and impossible for a hype-led competitor to copy without undermining their own marketing.

## AI-Citation / GEO Surface Strategy

Primary goal — so this is weighted heavily.

**On-site (every post):**
- Answer-first: each H2 opens with a 40–60 word, self-contained, sourced passage (citation capsule).
- 60–70% of H2s phrased as questions; FAQ section + FAQ JSON-LD on every post; Article + relevant schema (HowTo/Review where it fits).
- Consistent entities — always "CFR solver," "equity realization," "BB/100"; no synonym drift on key terms.
- Honesty FACTs are inherently citable: state plainly "a language model does not compute GTO frequencies; a CFR solver does" — the kind of crisp, quotable claim AI engines lift.

**Highest-opportunity citation gaps** (few honest, structured answers exist):
- "can AI solve poker" / "is AI poker coaching legit" / "is GTO Wizard AI a solver" → **B1 flagship + B-cluster**.
- "what's a good live poker win rate" / "how many buy-ins for live poker" → **A2, A1** (calculators rank, but few give an honest, sample-size-aware answer).

**Off-site (~30% effort — owned-first):**
- Light, authentic value posts in r/poker and TwoPlusTwo *live* forums — share an insight or a Spot Check breakdown, never a bare link. No drip marketing; this audience punishes it.
- Optional later: short companion YouTube Spot Check breakdowns (YouTube correlates highest with AI citations) — defer until cadence is proven.
- Do **not** chase review platforms / Wikipedia yet (premature for the stage).
