FAQ

Is TableLab free?

Yes. The core tracker, hand recorder, equity calculator, and dashboard are free. AI coaching has a generous free daily allowance.

Does it show ads or sell my data?

No ads, and we never sell your data. Your records are private to your account and protected by row-level security. See our Privacy Policy.

Is my data sent anywhere for AI coaching?

Only when you explicitly tap “Analyse”. At that point the relevant session or hand is sent to Anthropic (Claude) to generate the coaching, then cached to your account.

Can I export my sessions?

Yes — export to CSV/Excel any time from the in-app Import/Export screen, and import from other trackers too.
