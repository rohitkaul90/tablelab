TableLab

Poker bankroll tracker for cash games and tournaments.

TableLab is a free app that helps poker players track their results, study their hands, and improve their game over time. Log sessions, record hand histories, calculate equity, and get AI-powered coaching — all in one place.

Open App

Features

Session Tracking Log cash game and tournament sessions with stakes, buy-in, cash-out, duration, and notes.
Hand Recorder Record full hand histories street by street, with pot odds, bet sizing, and player actions.
AI Coaching Get Claude-powered analysis of your sessions and individual hands with actionable feedback.
Equity Calculator Run range vs. range equity calculations with GTO presets and Monte Carlo simulation.
Player Reads Track reads and tendencies on opponents you play with regularly.
Bankroll Dashboard Charts and statistics across your full history — win rate, BB/100, profit by game type, and more.

Platforms

  • Android — available on Google Play
  • Web — runs in any modern browser at tablelab.app

About

TableLab is built and maintained by an independent developer. All your session data is private to your account and protected by row-level security. The app does not show ads and does not sell your data.
