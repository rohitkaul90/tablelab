What is TableLab?

TableLab is a free poker bankroll tracker and study tool for live cash games and tournaments, available on Android and in any web browser. It brings together five things players usually juggle across separate apps: session and bankroll tracking, a street-by-street hand recorder, an offline equity calculator, opponent reads, and AI coaching that reviews your sessions and hands and tells you what to work on.

It's built for serious recreational and semi-pro players who play live and want to study away from the table — no spreadsheets, no desktop-only software, and no subscription to get started. Your data stays private to your account, and AI analysis is opt-in, sent only when you tap “Analyse”.

Everything in one place

📒 Session Tracking Log cash and tournament sessions with stakes, buy-in, cash-out, table size, duration, and notes.
🃏 Hand Recorder Record full hand histories street by street — from heads-up to 9-handed — then replay them on a table.

👁️ Player Reads Track tendencies on the regulars you face, and turn tags into rule-based insights.
📈 Bankroll Dashboard Win rate, BB/100, profit by game type, and trends across your full history.

How our analysis works

A language model is not a solver, and we don't pretend otherwise. TableLab keeps two layers cleanly separated, so you always know which is which.

The numbers are computed, not guessed: equity and pot odds from an on-device engine, and, where the spot is covered, GTO action frequencies from a real CFR solver (TexasSolver) we run offline and ship with the app. Those figures are handed to the AI as hard facts.

The AI's job is the words: it explains those numbers in plain English and reasons about the spots a solver can't tractably cover, like multiway pots and deep live decisions. It's barred from stating a GTO number it wasn't given, and it tells you when a read is a judgment call rather than math.

That separation is the whole basis for trusting the output. We wrote about why it matters in Can AI solve poker?

FAQ

Is TableLab free?

Yes. The core tracker, hand recorder, equity calculator, and dashboard are free. AI coaching has a generous free daily allowance.

Does it show ads or sell my data?

No ads, and we never sell your data. Your records are private to your account and protected by row-level security. See our Privacy Policy.

Is my data sent anywhere for AI coaching?

Only when you explicitly tap “Analyse”. At that point the relevant session or hand is sent to Anthropic (Claude) to generate the coaching, then cached to your account.

Can I export my sessions?

Yes — export to CSV/Excel any time from the in-app Import/Export screen, and import from other trackers too.

Does the equity calculator work offline?

Yes. The range-vs-range equity calculator runs entirely on your device using a Monte Carlo simulation with GTO preset ranges — no internet connection required.

Is TableLab good for tournament players?

Yes. You can log tournaments with buy-ins, re-entries, and stages, track ROI and ITM%, and use the built-in ICM calculator. Cash and tournament results are tracked separately so your stats stay clean.

Can I import from other poker trackers?

Yes. The importer reads CSV and Excel exports and includes presets for many popular mobile, desktop, and tournament-tracking apps, so you can bring your history with you.

Is TableLab a HUD or does it help during online play?

No. TableLab is a bankroll tracker and study tool for reviewing the games you play. It is not a real-time HUD or overlay and does not read or assist on any poker site during play.

What platforms does TableLab run on?

TableLab is available on Android via Google Play and as a web app at tablelab.app that runs in any modern browser. An iOS version is not available yet.
